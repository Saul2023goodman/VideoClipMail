## Brief and decisions

Student 001 is preparing 2027 practice-led doctoral supervision enquiries. The uploaded bundle supplies 60 distinct numbered supervisors, 11 universities, task subjects and per-task attachment requirements. The film follows the whole batch: prepared mail input → file classification → field normalization → reference-roster reconciliation → batch review → exact file resolution → institution-aware scheduling → execution queue → safe attachment replacement → reply-aware follow-up decisions.

The email bodies are prepared demo input derived from the provided subjects and student context. SmartMail imports and operates on them; the film must not imply that this extension generates AI-written enquiries. The roster is a sanitized XLSX derived from the uploaded master: names, reserved addresses, universities and research categories. Profile URLs and personal role descriptions are removed before capture. Transcript and MOI supporting samples are not uploaded.

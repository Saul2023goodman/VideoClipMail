## Product brief and execution decisions

An operator is inviting eight people at four organizations to a collaboration. A CSV export contains nine drafts, including two versions for Maya. The revised version still has a copied greeting. The operator checks the roster, keeps the correct version, repairs the greeting, matches the shared brief, spaces colleagues' invitations, updates three scheduled drafts when the brief changes, and responds to a human reply by stopping further automated follow-ups.

The goal is understanding: each native interaction must explain the problem that made it necessary and show its result. No detached feature slogans, feature title cards, stock footage, invented product UI, or replacement of the story with decorative motion.

## Visual and motion direction

Selected direction: a camera following an operator's work. Rejected directions: kinetic headline montage (repeats the original problem) and abstract mail particles (hides business logic). Preserve the native cool white workspace, blue accent, ink text, pale borders, tight data grid, and 12 px surfaces. Film narrative uses bundled Open Sans; native UI uses its original font. Background #f4f6f8, surface #ffffff, ink #182230, muted #667085, accent #1769e0, border #dfe5ec. No shake, flashing overlays, repeated glow, gratuitous whole-frame impacts, or unrelated dark cinematic skin.

Camera motion starts from the cursor-flyover recipe: independent cursor and eye, camera easing cubic-bezier(.55,0,.18,1), short 8% pullback on larger travel, click contraction .84 for three frames, 18-frame restrained click ring. The recipe's four-module demo is not copied as a feature tour. Its motion grammar is adapted to measured controls in the real app, with longer reading time and multiple meaningful operations within each section. Custom work covers the persistent video plane, scenario timing, crop follow, typing and scrolling. These are evaluated in motion, not claimed as a pixel match to a gallery demo.

Existing product tokens and the previous captured styleframes establish the brand. The revised story's native before/action/after proof frames serve as composition references before the final render. Native detail must be enlarged; captions are 56 px minimum and supporting annotations 32 px minimum. Camera stays frontal whenever text matters.

For export, the render script derives a local image cache from the two native videos. That cache is excluded from source distribution; the composition detects its presence and falls back to local native video when it is absent, including fresh workbench exports. Both routes use the same source frame, camera path and film graphics.

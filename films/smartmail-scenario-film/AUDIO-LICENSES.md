Open Sans files come from the installed fonts-open-sans package. Their Apache 2.0 license is included in public/fonts/LICENSE-OpenSans.txt. Application screenshot typography is rendered by Chromium's native fallback; no proprietary system font file is redistributed.

Selected cursor/camera motion grammar and layout-zoom coordinate math originate from https://github.com/Vincentwei1021/video-shotcraft at e1d15d91374490c59bbbc1a05c2da7efde429828. Gallery references are research material in references/ and are not incorporated as video footage. The published cursor-flyover preview uses an older dark dashboard; the current exact CursorFlyover.tsx uses porcelain modules. This film adapts its cursor/eye relationship and easing to the native workflow; it does not claim a visual reproduction of the old preview. Application surfaces come from the user's SmartMail Ops project; the film does not import artwork from Apple Preview.
